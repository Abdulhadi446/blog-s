Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Trillioniar Blog ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at blogs.thetrillioniar.me. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate this blog effectively:

  • Analytics data: We may collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited, browser type, and referring URLs through server logs.
  • Comments and submissions: If you submit content through our admin interface, we store the information you provide.
  • Cookies: We use session cookies to maintain admin authentication. No third-party tracking cookies are set.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the blog
  • Improve site performance and user experience
  • Generate aggregate analytics to understand readership
  • Manage admin access and security

3. Third-Party Services

This blog is a static site with no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of any future data collection

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thetrillioniar.me.
