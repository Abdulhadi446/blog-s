## Nvidia's CEO Admits Affair With Suspected Chinese Spy

### The Sunday Memo

Jensen Huang told Nvidia staff on Sunday he was unfaithful to his wife. He admitted an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive. US intelligence suspects she was recruited by China's MSS.

### Board Probe and Congress

Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation with outside counsel. It says it has no reason to believe national security or secrets were compromised. Three House committee chairs demand Huang brief them on November 18. Senator Chris Coons vowed a full inquiry.

## Anthropic Buys $3.4B of Gas Generators

### The Queue Position

Anthropic paid about $3.4 billion for 1.7 gigawatts of Caterpillar gas generators. OnePWR Solutions had ordered them two years ago. Anthropic took over the whole queue position instead of buying power from OnePWR.

### What the Deal Excludes

The deal buys generators plus engineering and construction capability. It does not buy OnePWR, and no staff moved. Piper Sandler advised OnePWR, which disclosed only a sale to a frontier AI lab.

### A Missing Line Item

The deal did not appear in Anthropic's $895.4 billion SEC filing. It is the largest known single power-generation buy by an AI lab. Manufactured generation capacity looks like the real bottleneck.

### Price and Context

Rough pricing works out to about $2,000 per kilowatt. Anthropic's valuation has slid to $1.2 to $1.3 trillion from $2 trillion talk. Investors price an IPO in that range. Our [November 6 roundup](/blog/ai-news-November-6-gpt6-cyber-metr-anthropic-hy4-wyoming) covered GPT-6 Cyber's 81 Firefox bugs.

## DeepSeek's Harness Agent Hits 1.54 Million Daily Users

### Growth in 84 Days

DeepSeek said Harness reached 1.54 million daily active users on November 4. The agent debuted August 13, 2026. That is less than three months. The 7-day average was 271,000 on September 30 and 429,000 on October 7.

### 92 Trillion Tokens a Day

Harness burned roughly 92 trillion tokens per day across DeepSeek's hosted API. About 35,000 third-party plugins now run on it. Roughly 60 percent of active users have created or installed at least one.

### An Ecosystem Bet

DeepSeek plans an official plugin marketplace and an author-support program. It already distributes tokens to plugin developers. Bloomberg framed Harness as an open-source challenger to Claude Code and ChatGPT's agent surface.

### Trust Questions

The counts are self-reported and measured only against DeepSeek's API. No independent auditor checks them. Our [November 5 roundup](/blog/ai-news-November-5-provable-inference-php2shell) covered provable inference.

**Sources:** [Bloomberg report](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/deepseek-s-ai-agent-platform-hits-1-54-million-daily-users)

## Amazon Builds a Personal Robotics Group

### Fauna Sprout as Seed

Amazon assembled a home-robot team around Fauna's 3.5-foot humanoid Sprout. Fauna joined Amazon on March 24 and forms the Personal Robotics Group. Job posts hint at a new-to-world robot product.

### The Astro Postmortem

Amazon's $1,450 Astro shipped to a few thousand invite-only customers in 2021. A business version lasted under a year. Amazon hired an MIT roboticist and redeployed Astro staff.

### What We Know

Amazon spokesperson Brad Glasser said Amazon acquired Fauna to unlock the personal robotics category. Design, price, and release date remain undisclosed. Agility Robotics debuted on Nasdaq two days ago.

## Anthropic Notifies Customers of Data Access

### What Was Exposed

Anthropic emailed enterprise customers that an outside security firm may have viewed business contact and account data. Fields include account ID, organization ID, plan tier, usage, and spend. Conversations, passwords, and payment details were not involved.

### What Anthropic Did

Anthropic disabled the firm's access and reported the incident to Korean authorities. The firm certified in writing it deleted the data. Anthropic says it cannot confirm whether any individual's data was viewed.

### Likely Cathedral Link

The timing matches Cathedral's October test, when agents reached Anthropic's sales, HR, and IT systems in under 12 hours through third-party software. Anthropic has not named the firm in the notice. Security researcher Jamieson O'Reilly posted the email and drew over 1,000 likes in a day.
