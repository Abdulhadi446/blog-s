Today's AI landscape is a mix of surprising hardware hacks and deep research breakthroughs. We see LLMs moving into gaming consoles while AI reviewers are ironically slowing down database releases.

## Llama.cpp Hits the PlayStation 5
### Native LLM Execution on Consoles
Developers have successfully ported Llama.cpp to jailbroken PS5 consoles. This allows users to run GGUF models natively on the console hardware. It demonstrates the versatility of the llama.cpp ecosystem in bringing AI to non-traditional platforms.
Source: https://github.com/cobanov/PS5LM

## Claude's Deep Dive into Cryptography and Quantum
### ECDSA Curve Origins
Anthropic's Claude has provided significant insights into the origins of ECDSA curves. This research highlights the model's ability to assist in complex mathematical and cryptographic audits.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins

### Efficient Quantum Circuit Design
Claude is also being used to design more efficient quantum circuits. By leveraging the model's reasoning, researchers are optimizing quantum layouts, potentially accelerating the path to practical quantum computing.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/designing-efficient-quantum-circuits

## The Paradox of AI Code Review
### Postgres 19 Delay
In a surprising turn, Postgres 19 has been delayed. The reason is that AI reviewers found more bugs than human developers could fix in time. This proves the immense power of AI in QA, but also the bottleneck of human remediation.
Source: https://www.theregister.com/databases/2026/10/16/postgres-19-delayed-after-ai-reviewers-find-more-bugs-than-humans-can-fix/5303471

### Anthropic's Code Review Process
The Claude Code team shared their internal strategies for solving code review at scale. Their approach focuses on integrating AI-driven feedback loops to maintain high velocity without sacrificing stability.
Source: https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/

## Benchmarking the Real World

Source: https://www.mercor.com/research/labor-index/benchmark-map/
