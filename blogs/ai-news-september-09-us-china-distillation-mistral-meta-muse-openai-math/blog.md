## US Government Warns of "Malicious" AI Distillation by Chinese Firms

### NSA, CISA, and FBI Joint Advisory AA26-251A
The NSA, CISA, and FBI issued a joint advisory on September 8, alleging that six Chinese AI firms—including DeepSeek, Alibaba, and Moonshot AI—have engaged in "aggressive, malicious, and targeted" distillation. The advisory claims these firms have extracted billions of tokens from US frontier models since late 2024 to improve their own capabilities.

### Targets and Tactics
The campaigns specifically targeted chain-of-thought reasoning and code generation in models like Claude, GPT, and Gemini. To bypass geographic restrictions, the firms allegedly used "transfer stations," effectively strip-mining the intelligence of US models to accelerate their own development.

## Academic War: OpenAI vs. NYU over Navier-Stokes Proof

### The Euler Blowup and the Millennium Prize
In a shocking development, NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge released a Lean-verified proof of finite-time blowup for 3D incompressible Euler equations. Shortly after, OpenAI claimed to have extended this result to the full Navier-Stokes equations, one of the six Clay Millennium Prize problems.

### Allegations of Preemption and Authorship
Buckmaster has accused OpenAI of "racing to preempt" his work, alleging that OpenAI's internal models may have accessed his private prompts. He further claims that OpenAI's research VP, Sébastien Bubeck, attempted to strip Alpöge from authorship due to his Anthropic affiliation. Bubeck has dismissed these claims as "false and inflammatory."

## Meta Launches "Muse" Personal AI Agent in the US

### Proactive Assistance and Secure VMs
Meta has introduced "Muse," a personal AI agent now available via web, iOS, Android, and WhatsApp. Unlike standard chatbots, Muse is designed to be proactive, helping users meet goals and suggesting ideas. For privacy, the agent operates within a dedicated Secure VM.

## Anthropic Faces Expanded Class Action Over "Claude Max" Claims

### Deceptive Marketing of Usage Caps
Anthropic is facing an expanded class action lawsuit in California. Subscribers to the "Claude Max" tiers ($100 and $200/month) argue that the company's marketing of "5x" and "20x" usage increases was deceptive, as actual delivered usage falls far short of these ratios.

### The 5x/20x Ratio Controversy
The amended complaint highlights that the "Max 20x" tier reportedly delivers only about 1.7x the weekly Sonnet hours compared to the "Max 5x" tier, despite the significantly higher price point. A motion-to-dismiss hearing is set for November 6, 2026.

## DeepMind's AlphaGenome Atlas Maps 9 Billion DNA Variants

### A Petabyte-Scale Genomic Resource
Google DeepMind has released the AlphaGenome Atlas, a staggering 1-petabyte dataset. It predicts the molecular effects of every possible single-nucleotide variant across approximately 9 billion positions in the human genome.

### Impact on Rare Disease Research
The resource is being provided for free to the scientific community. Early results from the Broad Institute have already identified variants tied to rare diseases, while UK Biobank researchers found 22% more non-coding associations with BMI.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is "AI distillation" and why is the US government concerned?
AI distillation is the process of using a larger, more capable "teacher" model to train a smaller "student" model. The US government is concerned because Chinese firms are allegedly using this to "strip-mine" US frontier models, effectively stealing intellectual property and capabilities to build competing systems.

### How much is Mistral valued after its latest funding round?
Following its €3B Series D led by Samsung, Mistral's post-money valuation is estimated to be above €21B.

### What is the "Navier-Stokes" problem in the context of AI?
The Navier-Stokes equations describe the motion of fluid substances. Solving them is one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems. The current drama involves whether OpenAI's AI successfully solved it and whether they preempted the work of human mathematicians.

### What makes Meta's Muse agent different from standard chatbots?
Muse is designed to be proactive rather than reactive, meaning it suggests ideas and helps meet goals without waiting for a prompt. It also runs in a Secure VM and integrates directly with shopping and health apps.

### What are the claims in the Anthropic Claude Max lawsuit?
The lawsuit claims that Anthropic's "Max" tiers promised significantly higher usage limits (5x and 20x) than what was actually provided to paying users, constituting deceptive marketing.
