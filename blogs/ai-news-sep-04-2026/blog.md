## PACE Surfaces Hidden Conflicts in User Requests

Scal3R, a new ECCV 2026 paper trending on Hugging Face, identifies that online 3D-reconstruction methods degrade on long video because "regressing poses relative to a fixed first-frame anchor forces extrapolation far beyond the training distribution." The authors reformulate the task as multi-reference relative pose querying via learnable tokens (~1% of parameters) injected into a frozen backbone, then add online pose-graph optimization with loop closure. They report >60% lower average absolute trajectory error on KITTI and SOTA across Virtual KITTI, Sintel, TUM-Dynamic, ScanNet and 7-Scenes, with training converging in 8 GPU-hours. **Source: aiweekly.co, Hugging Face**

### Details
- **Paper**: Scal3R — ECCV 2026 paper on Hugging Face
- **Problem**: Online 3D-reconstruction methods degrade on long video
- **Solution**: Multi-reference relative pose querying with learnable tokens
- **Result**: >60% lower average absolute trajectory error on benchmark datasets
- **Training**: Converges in 8 GPU-hours
- **Source**: [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/)
